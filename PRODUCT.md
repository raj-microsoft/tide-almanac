# Tide Almanac
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified React, Vite, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Motion, Anime.js and a copied MIT Magic UI component. Static GitHub Pages.
## Users
Assumption from the delegated brief: curious coastal visitors exploring tides, lunar cycles and the history of the North Sea, not mariners seeking navigation advice. No direct interview mechanism is available in this delegated task.
## Product Purpose
An interactive coastal almanac with tide clock, moon phase, illustrative sea-level chart and carefully sourced storm-surge history.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Synthetic data labelled at point of use; never for navigation or flood warnings. Accessible at 390px and desktop; reduced-motion support. Hero-first review. Motion owns state transitions; Anime.js owns only scroll/load reveal jobs. All motion under 400ms. No paid services, keys or signups. Manus skipped.
## Evidence on Hand
Guide 01 provided by coordinator. No live tide measurements. Historical content requires authoritative source links.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable inputs, adequate contrast, reduced-motion support, readable chart alternative.
